About Ethan

Ethan Eglip is a Filipino composer and arranger based in the Greater Seattle Area. He has composed works for various ensembles such as orchestras, wind ensembles, and percussion ensembles. Recent works of his include The Narrows, which placed 2nd at WMEA's State Solo and Ensemble Composition Division, Pastel Limoncello, written for the Seattle Symphony, and Saudade, a concerto for solo oboe and orchestra, which premiered in 2026 with soloist Brooke Lin.

He currently studies composition under Yonatan Ron, a graduate student at the University of Washington. He has also studied under Marcin Pączkowski for composition.

When not composing, Ethan enjoys using his musical talents to accompany as a pianist for various soloists and for pit orchestras. He has been the lead keyboardist for several theater productions at his high school, including Something Rotten, The Hunchback of Notre-Dame, and Into the Woods. He has also participated as a keyboardist for the 5th Avenue Theatre's Rising Star Production of Jesus Christ Superstar in 2026. Along with playing the piano, Ethan is also a music director for productions, including Jesus Christ Superstar, Into the Woods, and upcoming in The Wizard of Oz. He also participates in his school's wind ensemble, percussion ensemble, jazz band, and orchestra.


Ethan also accepts commission requests for new works or arrangements.
